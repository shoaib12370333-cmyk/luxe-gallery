The studio

Made by hand, meant to last on your wall.

Luxe Gallery began with a simple idea: a wall deserves more than a print behind glass. Every piece here starts as raw plaster and fiber composite, shaped in a mould, then hand-carved while the material is still workable — the ridges, the folds, the light and shadow are all done by hand, not printed on.

The result is a surface that changes with the light in your room throughout the day. No two pieces carve exactly alike.
